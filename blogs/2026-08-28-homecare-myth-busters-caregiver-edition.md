After 35 years in homecare, I’ve heard every myth under the sun about caregivers. They’re arguably the most critical people in the homecare ecosystem, so understanding what drives them is important. But too often the people theorizing about what caregivers need, want, or care about have never worked alongside one, or even spoken to one.
